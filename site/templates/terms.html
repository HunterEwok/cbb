{{t "Terms of the public servers"}}

{{t "The project runs a forwarder and a blind relay at %s that every group may use, with no account and at no cost. These are the terms of using them." (code "cbb.tools")}}

{{t "What they are"}}

  • {{t "The forwarder passes the encrypted packets of your group's tunnel between your devices away from home and your hub, when the internet cannot reach the hub directly."}}
  • {{t "The blind relay lets the devices of a group without a hub meet away from home. It joins their encrypted connections and cannot read them."}}
  • {{t "Both are open to every group. Nobody is refused for who they are, where they are or what they use it for, within these terms."}}

{{t "As is"}}

  • {{t "The servers are offered as they are, without any warranty and without a promise that they are up. They may stop, move or change at any time, and their software is the project's own, in its early development."}}
  • {{t "If your group depends on them, run your own forwarder or relay, or forward a port to your hub: %s." (link "https://github.com/HunterEwok/cbb/blob/main/docs/admin/self-hosting.md" (t "running your own servers"))}}
  • {{t "Your devices check the servers' keys, and what you copy stays encrypted between your devices whatever a server does."}}

{{t "A fair share for everyone"}}

  • {{t "Each address, network and provider, and each group, gets a share of the servers' memory, connections and work. When a share is full, the server refuses what is new rather than take anything from the groups already on it."}}
  • {{t "So a busy group or a busy network may be refused for a while; the others go on. How the shares are counted is public: %s." (link "https://github.com/HunterEwok/cbb/blob/main/docs/relay-budget.md" (t "the servers' budget"))}}

{{t "What is not allowed"}}

  • {{t "Attacking the servers or anyone through them, or loading them on purpose to keep others out."}}
  • {{t "Using them for anything the law forbids where you are or where the servers stand."}}

{{t "A source that breaks these terms may be refused, at the level of its address, network or provider."}}
