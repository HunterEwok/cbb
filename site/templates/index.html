{{t "Principles"}}

{{t "Encrypted end to end"}}

{{t "Each copy is sealed on the device that makes it, for your own devices alone, and signed. A base or a server passes it on and can neither read it, change it nor forge one."}}

{{t "Your network only"}}

{{t "At home the devices talk to each other directly. Away from home they meet through your base or a backup server that cannot read what you copy. Nothing goes through a cloud."}}

{{t "Paired, then encrypted"}}

{{t "A device joins your group once, by a QR code or a six-digit code. The devices talk only to the devices of the group, and only over TLS."}}

{{t "Keys in the chip"}}

{{t "In a group made on a phone, the phone holds the group's key in its security chip, and each signature with it asks for your face, fingerprint or passcode."}}

{{t "Passwords stay put"}}

{{t "What password managers mark as secret is not synced automatically."}}
