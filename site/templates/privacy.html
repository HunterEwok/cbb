{{t "Security and privacy"}}

{{t "What you copy goes only to your own devices. Here is what protects it, and what each server along the way can and cannot see."}}

{{t "Your clipboard"}}

  • {{t "There are no accounts, no cloud and no telemetry. Copy Beyond Borders does not report to anyone."}}
  • {{t "Every copy, its text, images and files, is encrypted end to end: the device that copies seals it for the devices of your group that read copies (X-Wing, post-quantum) and signs it. Nothing that passes it on can read it, change it or forge one."}}
  • {{t "Only devices you let in belong to your group, and they talk only to each other. Every connection is encrypted with TLS, and each device proves it is in the group."}}
  • {{t "A phone keeps its keys in its security chip (the Secure Enclave, Android's Keystore); they never leave it. A computer keeps them in its TPM or Secure Enclave when it has one, otherwise in files that only Copy Beyond Borders can read."}}
  • {{t "What password managers mark as secret is not synced automatically."}}
