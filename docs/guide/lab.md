# The lab

Copy Beyond Borders promises one thing: one encrypted clipboard for your phones and
computers. The lab is where it tries what else a group of your devices can do. Everything
here works and is tested in CI, but it is an experiment, not what 1.0 promises: it may
change, and some of it has not yet run on real hardware.

| Experiment | What it does | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Keyboards | an Android phone, a Linux computer or the Nano ESP32 types a text into a TV, a tablet, a console or a computer that has nothing of Copy Beyond Borders on it | [keyboards and TVs](keyboards-and-tvs.md) |
| TV remotes | a computer at home types into an Android TV, a Google TV or a Roku as its remote | [keyboards and TVs](keyboards-and-tvs.md#a-computer-as-the-tvs-remote) |
| The Arduino Nano ESP32 | a board of a few euros as a device of your group, and its USB port as a keyboard | [the firmware](../../esp32/README.md) |
| The ZX Spectrum Next | the Next copies to the group and pastes from it through the Raspberry Pi inside it | [`.cbb` for the Next](../../apps/zxnext/README.md) |

Tell us how an experiment works for you in an issue. What proves itself may leave the lab
and become part of what Copy Beyond Borders promises.
